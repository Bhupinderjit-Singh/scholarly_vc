# Scholarly

Scholarly is a "study together" web app. You start or schedule a private video study session, invite friends, and study with your cameras on so nobody feels alone at the desk. The app tracks camera-on study time against a daily target, keeps a history of sessions, shows upcoming sessions on a calendar, and (Phase 2) analyzes your own session recording to report focus, deep-work blocks, and distractions. The recording is yours alone and is deleted once the report exists.
